Academic Programs

Educational Faculties & Courses

Structured learning pathways spanning Quranic Memorization, Higher Islamic Jurisprudence, Women's Education, and Modern IT Skills.

Duration: 2 - 3 Years

Hifz-ul-Quran & Tajweed

Complete memorization of the Glorious Quran with precise phonetic rules (Tajweed) and daily revision protocols.

Duration: 8 Years

Dars-e-Nizami (Shahadat-ul-Aalamiyyah)

In-depth study of Classical Arabic Grammar, Literature, Islamic Law (Fiqh), Usul, Hadith Commentaries, and Tafseer.

Duration: 2 Years Post-Grad

Takhassus Fil Fiqh (Specialized Ifta)

Advanced specialization in Islamic Jurisprudence, research methodology, modern financial rulings, and edict writing.

Duration: 4 Years

Aalimah Program (Women's Campus)

Comprehensive Islamic studies tailored for female students in a secure, segregated, and dedicated facility.

Duration: 1 Year

Qirat As-Sab'ah (Recitation Certificate)

Mastering the seven canonical recitations of the Holy Quran under certified Qaris with connected Sanad.

Duration: 6 Months

Arabic Language & IT Skills

Conversational Classical Arabic combined with computer literacy, web design, and digital publishing for scholars.
